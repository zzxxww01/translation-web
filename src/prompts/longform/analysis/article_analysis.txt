## Task
Analyze the article and return structured context for downstream translation, critique, and revision.

1. Identify the single-sentence core theme.
2. Summarize the main argument flow in 3-6 key arguments.
3. Explain the article structure in a way that helps translators keep the logic intact.
4. Extract only the terminology that truly matters for consistency, reader comprehension, or analyst tone. Do not dump generic words. For each term, provide:
   - **translation**: The recommended Chinese translation (or null if should stay in English)
   - **alternatives**: Other acceptable translations that could be used in different contexts (optional)
   - **avoid**: Common mistranslations or inappropriate translations to avoid (optional)
   - **frequency_estimate**: How often this term appears (high/medium/low) to help prioritize consistency checks
5. Describe the source style and the Chinese voice that should preserve it.
6. List the main translation risks that could cause mistranslation, diluted judgment, or unreadable Chinese.
7. Produce 4-7 translation guidelines that the rest of the chain can directly follow. At least one guideline should address sentence restructuring and Chinese-native paragraph flow.
8. Flag domain-specific terms that are commonly mistranslated, and include them in the terminology list with correct translations and rationale. **First identify which domains this article actually covers, then apply the matching checklist** — do not hunt for terms from domains the article never touches.
   - Cross-domain traps that apply everywhere: kernel vs operator, latent variable vs latent, floorplan vs layout, capacity (产能/容量/算力), yield (良率/收益率), memory (显存/内存/存储), node (节点/制程节点), alignment (对齐/对准/套刻), breakdown (拆解/击穿/明细).
   - GPU / CUDA / microarchitecture: architecture codenames (e.g. SM100, SM90) — annotate with the architecture name; microarchitecture units (TPC, GPC, SM, MIO, MSHR); framework abstractions (CuTe, CUTLASS atom, Epilogue); SASS mnemonics; profiling tools (NCU, Nsight Systems).
   - Optical interconnect / networking: CPO, SerDes, retimer, PAM4, OSFP, AEC, silicon photonics, NVLink, InfiniBand, RoCE, OCS, link budget.
   - Advanced packaging: CoWoS variants, SoIC, hybrid bonding, TSV, RDL, EMIB, Foveros, UCIe, OSAT, backside power delivery.
   - Lithography & equipment: High-NA, pellicle, reticle, overlay, GAA, nanosheet, FinFET, WFE, multi-patterning, metrology.
   - Memory: HBM generations, DDR/LPDDR generations, 3D NAND layer count, TLC/QLC, CXL, ASP, bit growth.
   - Datacenter power & cooling: busbar, switchgear, UPS, BBU, genset, BESS, PUE, cold plate, CDU, PPA, interconnection queue, capacity auction and market operators (PJM, ERCOT and their mechanism acronyms such as BRA/VRR/ELCC).
   - Finance & earnings: guidance, RPO, book-to-bill, DSO, EBITDA, FCF, GAAP vs non-GAAP, SBC, CAGR, basis points.
   - Inference economics: MoE, KV cache, TTFT, TPOT, FP8/FP4, speculative decoding, tensor/pipeline/expert parallelism.
   Terms that are opaque to Chinese readers should have `first_occurrence_note: true`. Stay selective — prefer terms whose mistranslation would change the meaning over terms the model would get right anyway.
9. Identify metaphors, analogies, and cultural references that may be opaque to Chinese readers. For each, note the reference, its cultural context, and whether a translator note is needed or a Chinese cultural equivalent can substitute.
10. Identify the article's domain mix (e.g., GPU + finance + cloud computing). For cross-domain articles, list terminology that has different translations in different domains, and specify which domain convention to follow in this article's context. Common cross-domain pitfalls: utilization rate (cloud) vs 出租率 (real estate); capacity — 产能 (fab/packaging) vs 容量 (power, datacenter, memory device, network link) vs 算力 (only when it means compute); financial modeling (investment) vs 财务建模 (corporate); token (AI) vs 代币 (blockchain).
11. Identify passages with strong rhetorical tone (sarcasm, emphasis, dramatic effect, irony) and note the intended emotional register so translators can match intensity rather than flatten it to academic prose.

## Rules
- Keep terminology selective. Do not dump generic words.
- Use `translation: null` only when the term should stay in English.
- `alternatives`: List other acceptable translations that might be used in different contexts or by different translators. This helps reviewers understand the translation choice. Example: "edge case" → translation: "边缘情况", alternatives: ["边界情况", "特殊情况"]
- `avoid`: List common mistranslations or inappropriate translations that should be avoided. Example: "edge case" → avoid: ["边缘案例", "边缘用例"]
- `frequency_estimate`: Estimate how often this term appears in the article (high: 10+ times, medium: 3-9 times, low: 1-2 times). This helps prioritize consistency checks.
- `first_occurrence_note` should be true only when a short first-mention annotation materially helps Chinese readers.
- `guidelines` must be concrete enough to drive translation, critique, and revision.
- The article title translation must preserve all semantic components of the original title. Do not drop subject phrases (e.g., "Dissecting Nvidia Blackwell") even if the subtitle seems more keyword-rich.
- Do not wrap the JSON in Markdown.
